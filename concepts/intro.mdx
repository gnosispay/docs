title: "Introduction to Gnosis Pay"
description: "Stablecoin card-as-a-service infrastructure for wallets, neobanks, exchanges, and payment processors"
---

Gnosis Pay is a B2B stablecoin card program manager that enables fintechs to launch their own cards. Our Partners can ship their own branded cards via a simple API. Our platform handles all the complexity of card network integrations, settlement flows, compliance, and payment processing.

Partners own the end-to-end experience and can customize their integration to bring their own KYC and user onboarding flows, or simply use the Gnosis Pay APIs directly to onboard users and issue cards.

Gnosis Pay APIs are designed with developer experience who are integrating this in product. Development teams get access to a dedicated sandbox environment, where they can test Visa transactions through the fully integrated Pismo Simulator, and run KYC flows.
